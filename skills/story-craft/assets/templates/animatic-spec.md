# Animatic Spec — [Project Name]

> The animatic is the cheapest place to catch the expensive problems. Treat it as a real review gate. Its job is to validate STRUCTURE, PACING, and INTELLIGIBILITY before anyone invests in finished art and motion.

**Built from:** beat sheet v___ / storyboard v___  | **Temp VO:** yes/no  | **Temp music:** yes/no

## What the animatic must include
- Every storyboard frame, held for its intended duration.
- Temp narration (scratch VO or TTS) at real pace — pacing lies without it.
- Rough timing of major transitions (not polished motion).
- Placeholder on-screen text in final position (to check caption-safe zones).
- A pass of the intended music bed level under the VO.

## Review gate — answer before proceeding to production

### Structure
- [ ] Macro-arc reads end to end without explanation.
- [ ] Each beat is one idea; the causal chain holds (no "and then" sag).
- [ ] The hook lands in the first beat; brand/value early for persuasion.

### Pacing & segmentation
- [ ] Complex processes are segmented into 3–7s chunks with visual resets.
- [ ] No beat is so dense the eye can't keep up; none so empty it drags.
- [ ] Interactive pause/branch points (if any) fall at meaningful boundaries.

### Load
- [ ] No full-sentence on-screen text competing with busy visuals.
- [ ] Labels sit next to what they describe.
- [ ] Signaling cues guide the eye in dense frames.

### Intelligibility
- [ ] Temp VO is clearly audible over the music bed.
- [ ] The piece still communicates with sound OFF.
- [ ] Reduced-motion version would preserve all information.

### Accessibility / localization readiness
- [ ] Caption-safe zones clear; no overlap with on-screen text.
- [ ] Text is recoverable as live text for localization.
- [ ] No load-bearing meaning trapped in motion or culture-specific symbols.
